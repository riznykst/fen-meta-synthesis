# Industry commentary — SYNTHETIC

**SYNTHETIC. Written for the demo to illustrate a common attribution error. It is not
a real commentary, not a quote from any published source, and not legal advice.**

A frequent misreading of the AI Act concerns how long documentation must be kept.

1. The Act requires a quality management system for high-risk systems.
2. Article 11 requires the technical documentation to be kept for 10 years after the
   system has been placed on the market.
3. Providers should therefore plan a ten-year archiving period from day one.

The paragraphs above are intentionally illustrative: point 1 is a real obligation,
point 2 is the misattribution the demo checks, point 3 is advice you should not follow
without a lawyer.
