## Licence

EUR-Lex content is reusable under the Commission's reuse policy (Commission
Decision 2011/833/EU of 12 December 2011 on the reuse of Commission documents):
reuse is allowed, including commercially, provided the source is acknowledged and
the original meaning is not distorted. The acknowledgement above is the required
attribution.

## How the text was obtained

Official HTML of the act, retrieved 2026-11-02, reduced to the three articles;
whitespace (non-breaking spaces, runs of spaces) normalised. Wording unchanged.
Retrieval is scripted in the commit that added this dataset, so the extraction can
be repeated and compared.

## What is deliberately not here

- No legal interpretation, no advice, no claim of compliance with the Act.
- No recitals, no annexes — only the three articles used by the demo.
- The commentary is SYNTHETIC and is labelled as such in the UI.
